Who we are

Free weekly running in Berlin.

Berlin Harriers is an international, English-speaking running club. We run together in Berlin, on the track, the streets and in the parks, with several free, uncoached runs every week. Just turn up to any session and run with us.

There's a run for every pace and goal, from track sessions that help you build speed to social runs with no set pace. We also head out of the city regularly for trail running events.

Whether you're training for a race or worried you'd be the slowest in the group, you'll fit in. We run because we love it, and it's better with friends. Check our calendar to find the right run for you.

The schedule

Where to find us

Every run is open to everyone, members and first-timers alike. Each one ends somewhere with food or a drink, which is at least half the point.

Mon 🦆Monday Duck Next 18:30 · ~8 km · 5:45–6:30 min/km East of the Siegessäule, Tiergarten. An easy social loop through the park and along the Spree. There are bike racks at the meeting point, and in summer we sit by the water afterwards or head to the Biergarten am Neuen See.
Tue 🎽Tuesday Track Next 19:15 · 5–10 km · every pace Tuesday is the day to run fast. Sessions are built so you can join in whatever your speed, and no track experience is needed — everything gets explained. The meeting point is posted each week in Discord (opens in a new tab). Food and a drink afterwards.
Thu 🦢Thursday Swan Next 06:45 · ~7 km · relaxed Prisma Pavillon on the Landwehrkanal, for the early risers. A relaxed run along the water that finishes at La Maison for a pastry and a coffee.
Fri 🐕Friday Dachshund Next 12:30 · ~6 km · conversational Next to the DDR Museum in Mitte, at lunchtime. A conversational run along the Spree, into the Tiergarten and back down Unter den Linden.
Sat 🌳Saturday parkrun Next 09:00 · 5 km · any pace, timed We turn up at the Hasenheide (opens in a new tab) and Tempelhofer Feld (opens in a new tab) parkruns — free, weekly, open to all and run by own volunteers. Be there by 08:50 for the briefing. Coffee and cake in a nearby café afterwards.

Please note. Our runs are an informal invitation to run together — they are not organised events of the club and not coached or supervised sessions. There is no registration and no fee. Everyone takes part on their own responsibility and is asked to judge their own fitness and health. We run on public roads and paths, where the normal traffic rules apply and each runner is responsible for their own conduct. Guests who are not members of Berlin Harriers e.V. are not covered by the club's sports insurance.

Joining

You never have to become a member to run with us, and membership itself is free — no fees, no dues. Members get a vote at our meetings and a place on the trips and socials. Applying takes about two minutes and means signing our code of conduct.

If you were at our founding meeting, you are already a member and there is nothing for you to do.

Questions

Things people ask

The short answers. Anything else, ask in Discord.

Am I fast enough to join?

We have club members of many different speeds. Some of our usual runs are done at easy or conversational paces: we agree the pace depending on who is joining and try to stick together, so no one is left behind. On Tuesday and Saturday everyone runs at their own pace, we cheer each other on and grab a bite or a drink together afterwards. The Harriers is a good place to find people who run at your pace and meet up for runs, and a good place if you're looking to get faster, too.

Do I need to speak German?

No. Our members come from many different countries, and we are a primarily English-speaking club.

What if I am new to running?

We are happy to welcome members who are new to running. It makes us happy to see new people join this awesome sport we love so much. Come along to one of our runs, and we'll be glad to give you tips to help you on your way.

Where do I leave my bag?

Some of our runs take place in more organised settings, like Tuesday Track and the Saturday parkruns, but the other runs during the week have nowhere to leave a bag. We recommend a running backpack if you want to carry a warmer jacket, extra snacks or water.

Do runs go ahead in bad weather?

Generally we meet up rain or shine, but it's best to check the Discord channel for each run the day before or the morning of the run to make sure it's taking place. Runs are sometimes cancelled because of icy conditions.
