Run with us first

You never have to become a member to run with us — every weekly run is open to everyone. Becoming a member means officially being part of Berlin Harriers e.V.: you get a say in the club's future, a vote at our General Assembly, and a place on members-only trips and socials.

Who can join

To apply for membership, you should:

  • be at least 18 years old,
  • have come to at least three Harriers events in person in the last three months, and
  • intend to keep coming to in-person events regularly.

If you were at our founding meeting, you are already a member and there is nothing for you to do.

How to apply

Download the membership application, fill it in — on screen or on paper — and send it to committee@berlinharriers.de. The form asks you to confirm that you have read and agree to our code of conduct, so please read it first, along with our statutes. The committee then decides on your application and lets you know.

Free, and staying that way

There are no membership fees and no joining fees. That is written into our statutes, not just a current offer.

The club does have running costs — liability insurance, the website, snacks at events. The application form explains how to make a voluntary donation if you would like to help; around €15 a year per member covers them. It is entirely optional.

Leaving

You can leave at any time with 30 days' notice, by email to committee@berlinharriers.de. You are still welcome on every run afterwards.
