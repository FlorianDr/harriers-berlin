What to expect

We turn up at the Hasenheide (opens in a new tab) and Tempelhofer Feld (opens in a new tab) parkruns — free, weekly, open to all and run by own volunteers.

Be there by 08:50 for the briefing. Coffee and cake in a nearby café afterwards.

Learn more about parkrun (opens in a new tab) and sign up for an optional barcode to keep your personal times.

Hasenheide parkrun

Meeting point: Volkspark Hasenheide (map, opens in a new tab)

The course runs on a mix of different surfaces, including off-road sections. It is marked with signs and marshals, and includes going up and down a hill.

View route on Strava (opens in a new tab)

Tempelhofer Feld parkrun

Meeting point: Tempelhofer Feld (map, opens in a new tab)

The course runs fully on asphalt and is marked with signs and marshals. It takes place fully on the open-air airport field.

View route on Strava (opens in a new tab)

Please note. Our runs are an informal invitation to run together — they are not organised events of the club and not coached or supervised sessions. There is no registration and no fee. Everyone takes part on their own responsibility and is asked to judge their own fitness and health. We run on public roads and paths, where the normal traffic rules apply and each runner is responsible for their own conduct. Guests who are not members of Berlin Harriers e.V. are not covered by the club's sports insurance.
